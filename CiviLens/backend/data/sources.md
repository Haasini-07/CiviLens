# Dataset provenance and limitations

The `department_dataset.json` file is a curated keyword taxonomy for a local prototype. It is not a statistical dataset, does not claim measured department performance, and does not guarantee that a suggested department is legally responsible in a specific location. Department responsibilities vary by state and local government.

Reference sources consulted for the general classification and RTI guidance:
- Ministry of Home Affairs, Election Commission for UTs, “74th Amendment and Municipalities in India”: https://secforuts.mha.gov.in/74th-amendment-and-municipalities-in-india/
- Government of India, National Government Services Portal, RTI services and first appeal information: https://services.india.gov.in/service/detail/submit-a-first-appeal-for-rti-requests-1
- Department of Personnel and Training, RTI Online portal and guidance: https://rtionline.gov.in/
- Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs, CPHEEO: https://www.mohua.gov.in/ministry/our-division/details/cpheeo-QDM1IzMtQWa
- Government of Puducherry, Local Administration Department, municipal and panchayat functions: https://lad.py.gov.in/major-functions-lad

The prototype does not submit complaints or RTI applications to government portals. Users should verify the relevant public authority, applicable rules, fees, exemptions, and current deadlines before filing.
